# ANTISUYU: World & Locations

This document outlines the geographical and narrative structure of the game world. The world is divided into four major regions, connected seamlessly (or via hidden loading tunnels/caves). Each region has distinct visual traits, specific gameplay focus, and narrative significance.

## Region 1: The Cloud Forest (Eastern Slopes)
**Theme:** The Threshold, Overgrown History, Intrusion.
**Visuals:** Verticality, heavy mist, lush green vegetation reclaiming ruined stonework. Rain and mud. The lighting is diffused and atmospheric.
**Narrative Role:** The entry point. The player discovers the true nature of the quest here (the Quipu) and sees the violent methods of Sol Negro.

### Key Locations
*   **The Lower Blockade:** A muddy mountain road blocked by felled trees and makeshift barricades where the local Quechua community is protesting the Sol Negro operation.
*   **The Excavated Ruin (Santa Leonor Echo):** A recently unearthed provincial administrative center. Sol Negro has set up floodlights and heavy machinery here. Characterized by raw earth and damaged masonry.
*   **The Quipu Archive:** A hidden, intact chamber deep beneath the ruin, accessed by solving a knot-based puzzle. Dry, preserved, and structurally sound compared to the surface.

### Connections
*   **To High Sierra:** The path leads upward, following a steep, ancient staircase carved into the cliff face, eventually breaking through the cloud cover.

## Region 2: The High Sierra (The Navel)
**Theme:** Sacred Geometry, Altitude, Alignment.
**Visuals:** Stark, high-altitude puna (grasslands). Clear, piercing sunlight, deep blue skies. Exposed rock formations and massive, precise ashlar masonry (polygonal stones fitting perfectly).
**Narrative Role:** Tracking the pilgrimage route using ancient navigational tools. The midpoint revelation of Vargas's true motives occurs here.

### Key Locations
*   **The Qenko Solstice Marker:** A carved limestone outcropping with complex gnomons (shadow-casting stones). Used for a major light-alignment puzzle.
*   **The Chakana Gate:** A massive stone door set into a canyon wall, bearing the stepped cross. The site of the argument between Naira and Vance regarding its meaning.
*   **The Sayhuite Map Table:** An open-air plaza featuring a massive, carved boulder depicting a miniature landscape. Water must be routed through its channels to reveal the path forward.
*   **The Outpost (Mid-Game Confrontation):** A ruined military checkpoint overlooking the descent into the jungle. Stark, functional architecture. The site where Vargas reveals the mining concession plot.

### Connections
*   **To Cloud Forest:** The steep stairs descending back into the mist.
*   **To Jungle Lowlands:** The path goes underground through a massive *paqarina* (cave entrance), marking the descent into Uku Pacha.

---

## Region 3: The Jungle Lowlands (Uku Pacha)
**Theme:** The Womb, The Serpent, Instability.
**Visuals:** Bioluminescence, deep caves, subterranean rivers. The environment is vibrant and alive, not a dead tomb. Later, dense, sweltering jungle canopy with dappled light.
**Narrative Role:** The descent into the inner world. The player must navigate carefully to avoid triggering collapses, learning to move *with* the environment rather than against it.

### Key Locations
*   **The Serpent's Path:** A network of flooded caverns marked by Amaru (two-headed serpent) carvings. Requires managing water levels and understanding the dual nature of the serpent (creation/destruction) to progress.
*   **The Trembling Tunnels:** Unstable cave sections where Vargas's explosives have compromised the structural integrity. Navigation requires stepping on specific "safe ground" markers (Pachamama spirals).
*   **The Vanguard Choke Point:** A defensible cavern where Tayta Tomas and the community members have set up a barricade to hold off Sol Negro mercenaries, allowing Naira to proceed alone.

### Connections
*   **To High Sierra:** The *paqarina* entrance leading back up to the open air.
*   **To Paititi:** A hidden, underwater passage that eventually surfaces in the hidden valley.

---

## Region 4: Paititi (The Refuge)
**Theme:** Unbroken Lineage, Communion, The Sun.
**Visuals:** A pristine, sprawling city perfectly integrated into a hidden valley. Golden light, functional aqueducts, untouched ashlar masonry. It is not ruined; it is waiting.
**Narrative Role:** The climax. Securing the city, achieving the ambiguous solar communion, and defeating Vargas through environmental means.

### Key Locations
*   **The Outer Terraces:** Massive agricultural terraces that double as defensive walls.
*   **The Plaza of the Sun:** The central gathering space, dominated by an intricate golden disk (the *Punchao*). This is the site of the final puzzle.
*   **The Sanctuary:** The inner chamber containing the grand observatory mechanisms. It is designed to catch the light of the sun at a specific moment.

### Connections
*   **To Jungle Lowlands:** The underwater passage. Once Paititi is secured, the game ends or enters a post-game state here.
