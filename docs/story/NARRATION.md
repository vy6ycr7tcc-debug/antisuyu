# ANTISUYU: Narration Script
*(Naira's voiced field-journal entries. Tone: Analytical at first, increasingly humbled, intimate, spoken aloud like voice notes recorded on the move.)*

## Act I: The Cloud Forest
**Entry 01: The López Letter**
"I’ve read the López letter a hundred times. A Jesuit priest in Rome, sixteen-hundred, writing about a golden king deep in the Amazon. He never even saw it. It’s third-hand hearsay. But Sol Negro... they're treating it like a GPS coordinate. They’re tearing apart the cloud forest looking for El Dorado. It’s infuriating. They don’t care about the history, just the gold. I have to find whatever it is they’re looking for first, if only to keep it out of a private auction house."

**Entry 03: The Quipu**
"It isn't a map. The artifacts in this ruin... they aren't pointing to a city. It’s a *khipu*. A massive one. I found hair braided into the primary cord. It’s a signature. Someone from a royal *panaca* made this. They were recording an inventory, a relocation. Moving something massive away from Cusco. Away from the Spanish. The knots... the twist direction... it’s not about gold. They were hiding something *living*."

## Act II: The High Sierra
**Entry 07: The Ceque Lines**
"The coordinates track back to the Coricancha. The navel of the world. But it's not a straight line into the jungle. It follows the *ceques*. The ritual lines. I'm starting to think this wasn't an evacuation. It was a pilgrimage. They moved along the sacred geometry of the empire."

**Entry 12: The Chakana**
"Ran into a tour group today. The guide was confidently explaining that the Chakana represents the 'three worlds'—heaven, earth, underworld. I didn't have the heart to tell him that’s a modern invention. The Inca didn’t map it like that. It’s a bridge. An astronomical tool. When you look at the stones... when you really look at them... they aren't telling stories. They're doing math with the stars."

**Entry 15: The Reversal**
"I was wrong. Sol Negro was wrong. We reached the coordinates. There's no city here. No Paititi. It’s just an outpost. A waypoint. Paititi... it’s just the *Inkarri* myth twisted by colonial greed. The Spanish wanted gold, so they imagined a city of it. But the *khipu*... I finally translated the central cluster. They didn't hide a city. They hid *him*. Manco Capac. The stone mummy of the founder. And under Inca law, that mummy is a legal person. It owns the empire. If Sol Negro gets their hands on it..."

## Act III: The Jungle Lowlands
**Entry 19: Uku Pacha**
"We are going down. Deep into the cave systems. Sol Negro is using explosives, brute-forcing their way through. It’s making the ground sick. Every blast triggers a tremor. Pachamama isn't a metaphor for 'nature' down here, she feels like a living, breathing thing. And she is angry. They think they're descending into hell. But this is *Uku Pacha*. The womb. It feels... fertile. Sacred."

**Entry 22: The Amaru**
"The serpent carvings everywhere. The *Amaru*. The mercenaries shoot at them, thinking they mark traps. They don't understand. The serpent isn't a monster. It’s wisdom. It’s the guide between worlds. I followed the water flow, the path of the serpent, and the way just... opened. You don't fight the Amaru. You learn from it."

**Entry 25: The Wawki**
"They thought they had it. Sol Negro found a mummy bundle and celebrated. But it’s a *wawki*. A brother-simulacrum. A decoy. They are so blinded by the idea of 'treasure' they don't even know what they're looking at. The real Manco Capac is still down here. I can feel it. The air is too still."

## Act IV: The Sanctuary
**Entry 28: The Alignment**
"I found it. The sanctuary. Untouched. The Spanish never made it here. Manco Capac... he’s here. Turned to stone, just like the legends said. But he’s not a relic. He’s... he’s seated facing the grand window. It’s an observatory. Waiting for the sun."

**Entry 31: Communion (Final Entry)**
"The light hit the golden disk. I thought it was just a mechanism. A puzzle. But when the alignment completed... the sun. *Inti*. It wasn't just light. It was a presence. A conscious, breathing presence. The ancestors... they didn't mummify their kings to preserve the past. They did it so the dead could stay in the conversation. They are still here. History missed so much. Sol Negro wanted a prize to sell. They didn't realize they were breaking into a living home. The earth swallowed them, and I... I'm leaving him here. The nameless god has his sanctuary back."
